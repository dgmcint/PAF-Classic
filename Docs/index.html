PAF Classic

The familiar Personal Ancestral File, rebuilt for today’s computers. Free for Windows, Mac and Linux.

Download… GitHub
Features
  • Opens your existing PAF files
  • The family, pedigree and individual screens PAF users know
  • Saves to its own .pafc format, leaving your original files untouched
  • Your data stays on your computer, with no account and no ads
